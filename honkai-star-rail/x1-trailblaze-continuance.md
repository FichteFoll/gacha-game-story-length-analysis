# Trailblaze Continuance

**Region:** Worlds already visited | **Game versions:** 1.4 - 4.5 | **Entries:** 9 | **Estimated chapter length: 34 h 38 min**
<!--/gen-->

Interludes the wiki types apart from the Trailblaze Missions:
each one returns to a world the Express has already passed through
and tells a story around it while the main questline is elsewhere.
They count towards nothing in this report's total for the main questline,
and they take no part in its rankings,
so none of them is called the longest or the shortest mission in the game.
The report numbers them in release order, as the wiki does.

## Pacing

Of the <!--f:n_entries-->nine<!--/f--> continuances,
<!--f:n_above_3h-->seven<!--/f--> run past three hours.
The two that stay under it are both from the 1.x versions:
*<!--f:shortest_title-->Jolted Awake From a Winter Dream<!--/f-->*, the shortest, at <!--f:shortest_len-->2 h 11 min<!--/f-->,
