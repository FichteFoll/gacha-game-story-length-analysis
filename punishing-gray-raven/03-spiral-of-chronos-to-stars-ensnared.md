## Pacing

Nothing here is short.
The volume totals <!--f:total-->33 h 50 min<!--/f--> across <!--f:n_entries-->ten<!--/f--> chapters,
almost as much as the first twenty chapters put together,
and the spread inside it says as much about the uploaders as about the chapters:
a chapter of this length is read by some channels and skipped through by others.
Chapter 26 is the extreme case of what that does to the evidence.
Its pool holds no complete upload at all -
stage splits, streams and reaction videos, and nothing else -
so it carries no estimate,
and the volume total is short by whatever it would have been.
