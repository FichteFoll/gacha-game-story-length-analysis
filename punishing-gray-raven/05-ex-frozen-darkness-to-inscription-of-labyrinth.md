# Volume EX: Frozen Darkness to Inscription of Labyrinth

**Chapters:** EX-00 - EX-05 | **Game versions:** Frozen Darkness - Inscription of Labyrinth | **Entries:** 6 | **Estimated volume length: 13 h 15 min**

The six chapters the game numbers outside the main run.
Each shipped as the side story of a patch,
each is gated behind the chapter it sits between,
and the wiki files them under the main story all the same.
Unlike the numbered chapters of the same patches,
they are still uploaded whole,
so their pools are deeper than the chapters they shipped beside.

## Pacing

An EX chapter is a patch's side story:
a dozen or so stages, a normal branch and a hidden one,
and no boss gauntlet at the end.
<!--f:n_above_2h-->three<!--/f--> of the <!--f:n_entries-->six<!--/f--> pass two hours
and the rest do not come near it.
The longest is <!--f:longest_label-->Chapter EX-04<!--/f-->
at <!--f:longest_len-->4 h 06 min<!--/f-->,
which rests on three uploads and should be read as loosely as that implies:
an EX chapter's uploads divide into the ones that play both branches
and the ones that play only the normal one.
