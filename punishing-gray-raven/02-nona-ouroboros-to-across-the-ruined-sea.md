## Pacing

This is where the pacing changes shape.
Chapter 12 is the first chapter in the game to pass two hours,
and the wiki notes why it is built the way it is:
its content runs as several mission paths at once
rather than as one line of stages.
The longest of the ten, <!--f:longest_label-->Chapter 17<!--/f-->,
is <!--f:longest_len-->5 h 44 min<!--/f-->,
and it is not one chapter's worth of stages either:
the wiki writes it up as three named segments in a row.
Treat the figures from Chapter 17 on as softer than the ones above them.
The uploads behind them are fewer,
because a chapter this long is one most channels split.
