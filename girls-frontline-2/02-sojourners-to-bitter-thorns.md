The volume where the campaign menu starts counting in decimals.
Four of these <!--f:n_entries-->six<!--/f--> chapters shipped as named campaigns
filed between two whole numbers,
and three of them are the three shortest entries here:
*Sojourners of the Glass Island* is a Doll community's story told in Suomi's letters,
*Amidst Wings of Gray* and *Bitter Thorns and Daisies* are single-campaign chapters,
and between them Chapters 7 and 8 carry the Paradeus cult plot forward.

## Pacing

Three of the four decimal chapters come in under both whole-numbered ones,
and not because they are side content:
they hold fewer stages.
Chapter 6.7 has <!--f:parts_Chapter_6_7-->eight<!--/f--> of them, the fewest here,
and it is the shortest entry in this volume.
Chapters 7 and 8 carry on the launch run's climb,
Chapter 8 being the first entry in the game to pass four hours.
Chapter 8.7 is then the exception on both counts:
it has no more stages than Chapter 8.3
and is more than twice as long as it,
which is where the range says as much as the estimate does:
its uploads disagree about how much of the café to sit through.
