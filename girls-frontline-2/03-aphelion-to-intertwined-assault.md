# Volume 3: Aphelion to Intertwined Assault

**Chapters:** 9 - 12.5 | **Game versions:** not recorded | **Entries:** 5 | **Estimated volume length: 20 h 31 min**
<!--/gen-->

The story splits into two casts here.
*Aphelion* follows H.I.D.E. 404 along the edge of the Red Zone
and *Deep Oblivion* follows the NOMFA Dolls through the politics of Odesa,
while *Intertwined Assault* brings the Elmo back.
<!--f:n_entries-->five<!--/f--> chapters, and only the first of them is short:
this is the volume where an entry stops being an evening and becomes two.

## Pacing

Chapter 9 is the light one and everything after it is heavy.
It is the shortest entry in the volume,
an establishing chapter for a new squad,
and Chapter 10 is close to twice it.
The two *Deep Oblivion* chapters carry
<!--f:parts_Chapter_11-->24<!--/f--> and <!--f:parts_Chapter_12-->24<!--/f--> stages,
more than any chapter before them,
and are the reason this volume outweighs the launch run
on one entry fewer.
Chapter 12.5 closes the volume out
below both of the chapters it sits between.
