## Pacing

The launch chapters are the shortest in the game
and they grow almost monotonically.
Chapter 1 is a tutorial with <!--f:parts_Chapter_1-->ten<!--/f--> stages,
fewer than any whole-numbered chapter that follows,
and it is the only entry here under two hours;
Chapters 2 and 3 sit together around two and a half;
Chapters 4, 5 and 6 all pass three hours
without any of them reaching four.
The stage counts climb with the estimates,
which is the pattern the whole report follows:
this game's chapters are long because they hold more stages,
not because a stage got longer.
