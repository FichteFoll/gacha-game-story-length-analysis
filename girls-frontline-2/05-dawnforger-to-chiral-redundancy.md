**Chapters:** 18 - 21 | **Game versions:** not recorded | **Entries:** 5 | **Estimated volume length: 36 h 46 min**

and what starts as a trade delegation ends as a revolution.
Then Chapter 19.5 goes to an auction in Port Mica with the collab cast
and is a full-length chapter in spite of it.
*Chiral Redundancy* then brings back the Groza the Elmo lost in Chapter 4,
and its two chapters run longer than anything before them.

## Pacing

There is no easing in and no let-up:
every entry here is over four hours,
and the volume ends on the two longest chapters in the game.
Even Chapter 19.5, the volume's floor, sits above
almost everything in the first two volumes,
which is the clearest statement in this report
of how far the chapters have grown since launch.
Chapter 19.5 is the outlier in kind rather than in length:
a collaboration chapter, filed under a decimal number as Chapter 12.5 was,
which the game gave <!--f:parts_Chapter_19_5-->14<!--/f--> stages
and something close to the running time of an ordinary chapter.
*Chiral Redundancy* is the outlier in length.
Its two chapters carry <!--f:parts_Chapter_20-->22<!--/f--> and <!--f:parts_Chapter_21-->20<!--/f--> stages,
fewer than Chapter 15,
and still run longer than any chapter before them,
Chapter 21 the longest of all.
The uploads that show nothing but the campaign's cutscenes,
screened out here as not being playthroughs,
run longer than most whole chapters in the first two volumes on their own,
so the length is in the story stages rather than in the combat.
These are also the newest chapters here,
and most of their ranges span more than two hours:
read the ranges, not the estimates.
