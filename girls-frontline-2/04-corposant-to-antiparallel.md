# Volume 4: Corposant to Antiparallel

**Chapters:** 13 - 17 | **Game versions:** not recorded | **Entries:** 5 | **Estimated volume length: 28 h 28 min**
<!--/gen-->

Lviv, Project Eden, and the longest stretch of story in the game.
*Corposant* alone spans Chapters 13 to 15,
and the game numbered its middle one 14 rather than splitting the difference,
which is why Part 1.5 is a chapter here like any other.
*Antiparallel* then follows the Elmo out of the city and back to the Doll community.

## Pacing

This is by a wide margin the largest volume in the report,
and it holds the largest chapters in the game.
Chapter 13 opens it at about the length the previous volume was running,
then Chapters 14, 15 and 16 all pass six hours,
three consecutive entries longer than anything published before them.
Chapter 15 is where the stage count explains it:
<!--f:parts_Chapter_15-->27<!--/f--> stages,
more than any other chapter in the game,
staged as a chess game with a Knight, a King, a Witch and a Prophet
numbered through it.
Chapter 17 then drops back to about where Chapter 13 started,
and those two are the only entries here under six hours.
