## Pacing

<!--f:n_entries-->three<!--/f--> chapters, <!--f:total-->15 h 06 min<!--/f-->,
and nothing in the arc under four hours.
Chapters 8 and 9 sit inside a single band,
and their ranges overlap so heavily
that the order between them should not be read as settled;
Chapter 10 is clear of both.
Every figure here is measured against a pool
in which most uploaders published the chapter in halves,
so the accepted uploads are the few who did not.
