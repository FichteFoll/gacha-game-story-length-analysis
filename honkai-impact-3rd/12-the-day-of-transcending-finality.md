# The Day of Transcending Finality

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** 6.0 - 6.4 | **Entries:** 5 | **Estimated arc length: 10 h 31 min**
<!--/gen-->

Elysium Everlasting takes Mei through the Flame-Chasers' last secret
and out the other side alone.
What follows is the end of Part 1:
the world ends, the truth is named,
the moon's origin and finality is explained,
and the survivors turn towards tomorrow.
