# Taixuan Dream

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** 4.3 - 4.5 | **Entries:** 3 | **Estimated arc length: 6 h 08 min**
<!--/gen-->

Fu Hua left her body an empty shell when she put her sentience into a feather,
and in the dark of that shell something else has begun to think.
The arc is the phoenix's, from the immortal one to the wings that reawaken,
and it ends with her facing herself.

## Pacing

The point in Part 1 where a chapter becomes an afternoon rather than an evening.
Chapter XX is the arc's set piece at <!--f:len_Chapter_XX-->2 h 52 min<!--/f-->,
Chapter XXI is not far behind it,
and Chapter XXII closes the arc in <!--f:len_Chapter_XXII-->1 h 01 min<!--/f-->.
The wiki numbers the acts of these three chapters without naming them,
so no quest parts are published for them.
