# From the Deep Ocean

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** 3.0 - 3.4 | **Entries:** 4 | **Estimated arc length: 6 h 47 min**
<!--/gen-->

The Great Eruption has come and gone,
and both Schicksal and Anti-Entropy are looking for Kiana under the sea.
The arc runs from the deep phantom to the plain of Vigrid,
with a side trip into the Void Heavens along the way.

## Pacing

The first arc where the chapters stop being an evening's work.
Chapter XI is the longest of them at <!--f:len_Chapter_XI-->2 h 36 min<!--/f-->,
and only the EX chapter still fits inside an hour;
the arc totals <!--f:total-->6 h 47 min<!--/f-->.
The EX chapter is the odd one out in the evidence too:
it is short, uniformly titled and well covered,
where its neighbours are split across several uploads by most channels.
