# Remaining Flames

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** 4.7 - 4.9 | **Entries:** 3 | **Estimated arc length: 10 h 52 min**
<!--/gen-->

With the Herrscher of Sentience loose, Anti-Entropy, Schicksal and World Serpent
call a truce without saying so.
Seele and Bronya leave the Sea of Quanta for a new life at the Salt Lake base,
and the arc is what finds them there.
