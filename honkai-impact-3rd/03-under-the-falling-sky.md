# Under the Falling Sky

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** not recorded - 3.0 | **Entries:** 5 | **Estimated arc length: 4 h 42 min**
<!--/gen-->

Otto makes his move: Fu Hua is ordered to bring Kiana to Schicksal HQ by force,
the sword of rebellion is lifted against him,
and what wakes at the end of it is the Herrscher of the Void.
Two EX chapters follow the fallout into Arc City,
where World Serpent is already hunting.

## Pacing

The arc where the game's chapters stop being uniform.
Chapters VII and VIII are short,
and then Chapter IX runs to <!--f:len_Chapter_IX-->1 h 57 min<!--/f-->,
longer than the two of them together and the largest entry in the arc.
The EX chapters are separate entries with their own upload pools,
and they are measured here as the wiki lists them,
which is why the arc holds more entries
than it does numbered chapters.
