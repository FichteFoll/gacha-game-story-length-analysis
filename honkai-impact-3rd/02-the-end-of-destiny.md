# The End of Destiny

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** not recorded | **Entries:** 4 | **Estimated arc length: 2 h 46 min**
<!--/gen-->

Schicksal and Anti-Entropy both want the Gem of Desire,
and the chase for it takes the Valkyries from the wind of Wotan's dirge
to the underground prison beneath the Tower of Babylon.
It closes on the simulated battlefield of the 2nd Eruption,
where the 2nd Herrscher is waiting with a face Kiana wants to see.
