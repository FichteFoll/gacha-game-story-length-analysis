# Beyond the Stars

**Part:** Part 1.5 | **Game versions:** 6.9 - 7.2 | **Entries:** 4 | **Estimated arc length: 10 h 13 min**
<!--/gen-->

The twin bubble universe turns out to be where Sa, the Abyss of the Sea of
Quanta, has been drawing its energy, and the partner who brought them there
turns out to be Sa's representative.
Seele takes the rules of the universe for herself and becomes the Herrscher of
Rebirth; Fu Hua goes missing and finds an old friend.
The arc closes on the short bridge that leads into Part 2.
