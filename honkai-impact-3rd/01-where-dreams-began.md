# Where Dreams Began

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** not recorded | **Entries:** 2 | **Estimated arc length: 1 h 17 min**
<!--/gen-->

An unmanned Anti-Entropy battleship, the Selene, is on course for Sapphire,
and three St. Freya Valkyries are sent to board it before it arrives.
Kiana, Mei and Bronya take the ship;
what they bring back with them is a voice in Kiana's dream
claiming to be the 2nd Herrscher.
These are the chapters the game teaches itself with.

## Pacing

The arc the game opens on, and the lightest of them:
<!--f:total-->1 h 17 min<!--/f--> for the pair.
Chapter I is the tutorial and barely a chapter at all;
Chapter II is where the game stops explaining and starts telling,
and it is the longer of the two at <!--f:len_Chapter_II-->49 min<!--/f-->.
Both rest on wide pools, because these are the chapters
every new playthrough series starts with,
and their estimates are correspondingly firm.
