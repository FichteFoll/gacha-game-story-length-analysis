meets the Thirteen Flame-Chasers and follows them

## Pacing

Three chapters, close in size, for <!--f:total-->6 h 04 min<!--/f-->.
This is one of the worst-covered arcs in the report:
its chapters are long, its uploaders split them across videos,
and *Fur Elysia* in particular rests on a handful of complete uploads.
Treat the figures here as the order of magnitude they are.
