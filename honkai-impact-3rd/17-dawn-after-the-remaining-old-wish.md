# Dawn after the Remaining Old Wish

**Part:** Part 2 | **Game versions:** 7.8 - 8.4 | **Entries:** 7 | **Estimated arc length: 25 h 29 min**
<!--/gen-->

Dreamseeker goes back into the system pulled by the need to find Senadina,
and Vita suspects something in the system is doing the pulling.
Theresa joins them, and what they walk into is a Langqiu with no calamity in it,
the Seven Shus living ordinary lives,
until Vita starts a panic in Oxia and a familiar face surfaces in the middle of it.

## Pacing

By a distance the largest arc in the report:
<!--f:n_entries-->seven<!--/f--> entries and <!--f:total-->25 h 29 min<!--/f-->,
which is more than the whole of Part 1's first six arcs put together.
<!--f:n_above_3h-->six<!--/f--> of its entries run past three hours,
and *<!--f:longest_title-->If Destiny Concludes Today<!--/f-->* is the longest entry in the game
at <!--f:longest_len-->5 h 37 min<!--/f-->.
The two EX chapters are the light relief,
and *To None May God Pray* is the only entry in the arc under two hours.
Coverage is good throughout:
these versions shipped recently enough
that a whole-chapter upload is the normal thing to publish.
