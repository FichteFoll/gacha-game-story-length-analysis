# Tides of Time Gone By

**Part:** Part 2 | **Game versions:** 7.3 - 7.6 | **Entries:** 4 | **Estimated arc length: 11 h 19 min**
<!--/gen-->

Part 2 opens on Mars.
Valkyries Coralie and Helia cross paths with the data life Dreamseeker
during an investigation, and a Martian crisis surfaces under them:
the Seven Shus of Langqiu are holding off a shadow calamity
with the Astral Ring and whatever else they have.
