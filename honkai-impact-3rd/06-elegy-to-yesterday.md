# Elegy to Yesterday

**Part:** Part 1 | **Game versions:** 3.8 - 4.0 | **Entries:** 3 | **Estimated arc length: 4 h 17 min**
<!--/gen-->

World Serpent is about to swallow Nagazora in an eruption
to run a stigma experiment at city scale.
Kiana pushes the engine into the night sky, saves the city
and falls out of it;
Mei reaches her a step too late.
