into a frontier the Blight has already taken,
and then walks west across all of it,
from the dark Hub Base to the Æther Test Site under the rift.

## Pacing

The chapter's weight sits in *The Broken Lands* and *Path of Ascension*,
its two longest entries,
<!--f:parts_Process_I-->eight<!--/f--> and <!--f:parts_Process_III-->ten<!--/f--> missions apiece
and <!--f:len_Process_I-->2 h 38 min<!--/f--> and <!--f:len_Process_III-->2 h 37 min<!--/f--> on the clock:
both are overland crossings on foot with a set-piece fight at the end,
and they are <!--f:n_above_2h-->two<!--/f--> of the <!--f:n_entries-->five<!--/f--> entries here
to run past two hours.
Everything between them is short,
and short for two different reasons.
The prologue is a scripted corridor that cannot be played slowly.
*The Turbid Heavens* is the odd one out among the numbered processes,
at under an hour:
<!--f:parts_Process_II-->three<!--/f--> missions,
one of which is the AIC factory tutorial that the rest of the game runs on,
and uploaders either build the thing out or hurry past it,
which is why its uploads disagree so widely.
