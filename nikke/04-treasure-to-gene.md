## Pacing

The flattest stretch of the report.
Most of the volume sits between one hour and two,
the expedition format having settled into a rhythm,
with Inheritance and Gene running past that.
It is also the volume whose ratings hold up worst,
and for a reason particular to this game:
a crystal-region map can be walked or it can be cleared,
its uploads divide between the two,
and Advance rests on a handful of them either way.
