## Pacing

The onboarding volume, and the numbers say so:
no entry in it reaches the hour,
and the shortest entries in the whole report sit here.
Chapter 00 is a tutorial with a story attached,
Chapter 03 is a single errand,
and the chapters grow steadily around the hunt for Chatterbox,
which Chapter 04 closes as the largest entry of the ten.
The pools are large and the spreads are wide anyway:
a map this short doubles in length
if the uploader stops to clear every battle on it.
