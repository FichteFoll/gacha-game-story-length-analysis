## Pacing

Two chapters that could have come out of the opening volume,
and then the campaign changes gear.
Journey is the first entry in the game to pass the hour,
and the rest of the volume settles on either side of it.
Chapter 18 is the largest of the ten,
the staged defence of the Ark with a boss ladder in it.
Nothing here reaches two hours.
