## Pacing

The volume where the campaign stops being a series of missions
and becomes an expedition,
and half of its chapters pass the hour and a half.
Banishment is the largest of them,
the aftermath of the invasion told across the whole Ark.
The crystal-region chapters that close the volume
come out much of a size with each other,
because they are all the same kind of thing:
walk further in, find something worse.
