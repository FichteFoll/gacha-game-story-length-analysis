# Chapter II: Even When Divinity Remains Silent

**Region:** Rinascita | **Game versions:** 2.0 - 2.8 | **Entries:** 17 | **Estimated chapter length: 36 h 27 min**
<!--/gen-->

Rinascita is a country of carnivals, gladiators and drowned gods,
and the Dark Tide underneath it has been rising for a long time.
Rover arrives as a guest of the Order,
gets caught between Cartethyia, the Fisalia and the Fractsidus,
and leaves having killed a Threnodian and a great deal else.

## Pacing

By far the largest chapter here,
and the one where the game changes scale:
<!--f:n_above_2h-->nine<!--/f--> of its <!--f:n_entries-->17<!--/f--> entries
run past two hours, and <!--f:n_above_3h-->three<!--/f--> past three.
The pattern is that a version ships one long act and one short piece around it,
so the prologue, the two afterstory segues and *Rust, Sword and the Sun*
all sit under an hour and a quarter,
while the acts they bracket keep climbing.
Act XI is the chapter's set piece and the longest act in Rinascita
at <!--f:len_Act_XI-->4 h 23 min<!--/f-->:
the Dark Tide converges, and the game spends the whole of it there.
