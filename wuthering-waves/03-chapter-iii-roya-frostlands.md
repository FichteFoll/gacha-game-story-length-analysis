# Chapter III: To the Stars Yet to Shine

**Region:** Roya Frostlands, Lahai-Roi | **Game versions:** 3.0 - 3.4 | **Entries:** 14 | **Estimated chapter length: 26 h 31 min**
<!--/gen-->

Lahai-Roi has no Sentinel and a Void Storm where its sky should be,
and it answers that with an academy, a research collective
and a plan to fly out of the problem entirely.
Rover enrols as a student, loses a frequency, finds a ghost in a lecture hall,
and ends up piloting the thing the whole world has been building.

are numbered acts; the rest are a prologue, a crossover side story and segues,
and the segues are what makes the chapter hard to summarise in one number:
